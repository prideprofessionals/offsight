The spaces

Two rooms. One glass partition.

Book the boardroom on its own for a focused meeting or open the glass partition and use the whole space for training, workshops and events. A full-length curtain gives the boardroom privacy when you need it.

The Boardroom

A private boardroom for up to 10 people, with a large display, camera and soundbar for virtual and hybrid meetings, and sheer-curtained windows for natural light. Ideal for board meetings, leadership sessions and client presentations.

  • Up to 10 seated
  • Display and video conferencing
  • Half day, full day or evening
  • From $450 half day

The Entire Space

The boardroom and workshop area together, exclusively yours. The workshop area has three tables that push together into a laptop station for 6 or a monitor station for 3, or split into three tables of four for group work. There's a lounge next to the phone booth for breaks and calls.

  • Up to 22 seated, or 30 for cocktail-style events
  • Exclusive use of the space
  • Half day, full day, evening or multiple days
  • From $850 half day

FAQ

Good to know

Can't see your question? Email bookings@offsight.au and we'll get back to you within one business day.

Can I hire the boardroom for half a day?

Yes. Both the Boardroom and the Entire Space can be booked for a half day, full day, evening or multiple days.

Is there video conferencing?

Yes. The boardroom has a large wall-mounted display with a camera and soundbar for hybrid meetings, plus fast Wi-Fi throughout.

Where is offsight. located?

We're at 2/368 Rokeby Road, Subiaco, ten minutes' drive from the Perth CBD and a short walk from Subiaco train station.

How much does it cost?

The Boardroom starts from $450 for a half day and $750 for a full day. The Entire Space starts from $850 for a half day and $1,400 for a full day. All prices include GST.

What layouts can you set up?

The boardroom seats 10. In the workshop area, three tables push together into a laptop station for 6 or a monitor station for 3, or split into three tables of four. With the partition open, the entire space seats 22 for training or holds 30 for cocktail-style events.

Can you arrange catering?

Yes. We work with Wholefood Circus for morning tea, lunch and afternoon tea, and can cater for dietary requirements. You're also welcome to bring your own.

Can we have drinks at the end of the day?

Yes. There's a fridge with soft drinks, beer and wine.

What time can we get in?

Half days run 8am to 12pm or 1pm to 5pm, full days 8am to 5pm, and evenings 5.30pm to 9.30pm. If you need extra time to set up, let us know when you enquire.

How do we get in? Will someone be there?

You'll receive a door PIN before your booking so you can let yourself in, and a contact number if you need anything on the day.

Is there parking?

There's free 3-hour street parking on Rokeby Road and paid parking on nearby streets, plus public car parks within a short walk.

What's your cancellation policy?

A 50% deposit confirms your booking. Cancel more than 14 days out for a full refund. Cancellations within 14 days forfeit the deposit, and within 48 hours the full fee applies. See our Booking Terms & Conditions for details.
